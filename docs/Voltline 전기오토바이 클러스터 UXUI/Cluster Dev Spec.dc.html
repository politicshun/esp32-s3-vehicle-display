Voltline

Instrument cluster — LVGL implementation spec

800×480 landscape · 5 in and 7 in panels · ESP32-S3 + LVGL v9 core widgets · touch only
Rev A
Design source:
Voltline Cluster.dc.html

This document is the build contract for the cluster UI. Every number here is measured from the interactive mockup and is normative: geometry in device pixels, colour in hex, type in px. Where a value is a policy rather than a pixel (thresholds, update rates, motion budget) it is marked as such. Anything not specified here is the implementer's choice, but must not move a fixed element or change a signal colour.

1 · Hardware and canvas

Framebuffer800 × 480 px, landscape, RGB565. Identical for both panel sizes.
Panels5.0 in ≈ 188 ppi (1 px ≈ 0.135 mm) · 7.0 in ≈ 133 ppi (1 px ≈ 0.191 mm)
Touch targetsMinimum 56 px on the 5 in panel, 48 px on the 7 in panel (≈ 7.6 mm / 9.2 mm). Applies to mode pill, buttons, sliders, switches, DTC chip, page dots' hit area.
Safe area8 px inset on all four edges. No text, glyph or border inside it.
Band structureTop rail 44 px · gap 8 · content 360 px · gap 8 · status row 44 px. Fixed on every screen; the rail and status row never move or unmount.
ScrollingNone, anywhere. Content that does not fit is a design bug, not a scroll case.

6 · Data contract

Fault copy pattern: plain-language title / one-line rider consequence / mono code. Never "error", never an apology. A fault always states what the rider should do.

8 · Motion budget

AnimationFullLite (fallback)
Bar cell recolour, lamp on/off250 ms linear / 80 mssnap
Numeral colour change260 mssnap
Tile change (swipe)300–400 ms slidecut
Speed glow behind the numeralopacity tracks speed, 250 ms fadeoff — drop this first if frames are short
Turn / caution blink800 ms / 1200 ms hard step in both modes — never disabled, never eased

Easing: cubic-bezier(.2,0,0,1) standard, (.33,1,.68,1) for numeric settle. Nothing bounces, nothing springs, nothing loops apart from the two legal blinks. Pick Full or Lite per board after measuring the real frame rate — the mockup has a switch for side-by-side comparison.

Acceptance checklist

  1. Rail, content and status bands measure 44 / 360 / 44 px inside an 8 px safe area on both panels.
  2. No element moves between tiles: clock, telltales, pack V, motor, controller, dots and status word stay pixel-identical.
  3. Every readout renders its full range without wrapping or clipping — verify 199 km/h, 100 %, 999 999 km odo, 112 °C motor, 96 °C controller, 14.0 kW with a fault active.
  4. Day and night themes swap values only; no widget moves, no signal colour changes.
  5. Turn blink is a hard 800 ms step; unlit telltales sit at 18 % opacity and are never hidden.
  6. Plugging in forces the Charging tile; unplugging returns to Ride.
  7. A stale signal shows —, never a frozen value.
  8. Sustained ≥ 30 fps while riding with Motion full; otherwise ship Lite.

Open item before homologation: the telltale glyphs here are drawn to ISO/ECE conventions but are not certified artwork. Replace them with licensed ISO 7000 / ECE symbols in the production build.
